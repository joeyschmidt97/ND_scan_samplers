# Target-specific MGKDB study — frozen 2026-10-21

User authorized diagnosis, bounded model optimization and preparation of sampler/ablation testing. This is an additional development study, not a replacement for the first registration.

Data: same raw files and pre-run feature allowlist as mgkdb_regression.py. Development remains Hatch global. Existing pscans3 outcomes are used ONLY for descriptive diagnosis of the locked first-study predictions; no model is tuned or ranked by their new performance. Reserve the entire pscans2 campaign (448 raw rows) for eventual sampler grading: do not load its target values into scoring or selection. It has historical archive analyses, so call it unexposed to this regression study, not globally pristine. No external campaign result will be reported during this model search.

Clean development as before: finite targets, gamma != -1, finite numeric inputs with train-fold imputation. Keep directory groups and join groups sharing exact feature signatures. Remove development feature signatures matching either held-out campaign. No random-row fallback. Save row IDs, group assignment and data hashes. Feature/target transforms fit on training folds only. Signed omega is preserved. Original units cannot be independently validated from these chunks; cross-campaign normalization is an unresolved limitation.

Diagnostics on old pscans3 predictions: per-target RMSE/MAE by true mode, radius, frequency sign and training-support distance quartile; top 10% absolute-error share of total squared error; sign agreement; per-feature missingness, constant columns and fraction outside development min/max; same-input target disagreement. These describe an already-inspected test and cannot confirm a newly selected model.

Search: 20 GroupShuffleSplit outer splits (20% groups, seeds 0..19), 3 GroupKFold inner folds. For each target independently select minimum mean inner normalized RMSE, then score only that selected configuration on its outer validation. Fixed candidate order breaks ties. Final per-target candidate is selected by pooled inner-fold scores across all 20 outer splits; save lock before any subsequent grading. Outer estimates assess a selection procedure on repeatedly overlapping development groups, not 20 independent physics cases. Freeze configurations for later learning curves; do not interpret tuning minimum as confirmatory superiority.

Candidate field, single CPU thread: ExtraTrees 160 trees leaf=1 or 4 at max_features=1, and leaf=4 at max_features=.75; HGB learning_rate=.05, 200 iterations, leaves=15 or 31, l2=1, early_stopping=False; signed-asinh versions of extra4 and hist15 (transform y by asinh(y/training std), invert before original-unit scoring); Matérn GP nu=.5,1.5,2.5 with standardized inputs, scalar lengthscale initialized sqrt(d), optimized bounds .1..100, alpha=1e-4, no optimizer restarts, deterministic cap of 512 training rows; and omega-only sign-classify-then-regress with ExtraTrees classifier and leaf=4 experts, soft probability mixture. Gate classes are omega<=0 vs >0; only training labels construct the gate, test regimes are never supplied. Also score outer training-mean baseline. GP cap is a computational limitation, logged; it applies identically in later sampling tests above 512 rows. No TabPFN checkpoint download or model installation in this bounded field.

Primary per-target normalized RMSE (training-fold std); also original RMSE, MAE, R2. No gamma/omega composite selection. Save every inner score and selected outer score; fail visibly rather than omit a failed candidate. No claimed interval coverage: these models do not share a calibrated probabilistic output. Quantify uncertainty by directory-level analysis, not treating rows or repeated splits as independent trials.

Deliverables: diagnosis.json, inner_scores.json, outer_scores.json, selected_models.json, trained target-specific models, preprocessing/model settings, status and logs. No external holdout is scored. Full sampler experiment remains gated on the adequacy of outer validation and a freeze of acquisition settings.

Arms random, space-filling, VURS, GP uncertainty nu=.5/1.5/2.5 and fixed gradient/blend references; pin real adapter versions first.
